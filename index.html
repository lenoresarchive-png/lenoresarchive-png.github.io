Whether you are here for a review, a recommendation, a quiz or simply because you love getting lost inside a book, there is something waiting between these pages.

✦

Why This Archive?

Literature can be more than a school subject. A novel can teach us how language works, how characters are constructed, how themes grow through a story and how writers quietly create meaning.

The reviews here are especially designed to be useful for students: to explore why a novel might be worth studying, what can be learned from it, how it can enrich vocabulary and how reading closely can develop literary analysis.

Most importantly, I want that process to remain curious, enjoyable and enriching rather than mechanical.

About the Author

"And the Raven, never flitting, still is sitting, still is sitting"

— Edgar Allan Poe, The Raven

Hello, I'm Sharanya Mridha.

I have always been drawn to the strange little spaces that exist between words — the things a story says quietly, the questions it leaves behind and the feelings that remain long after the final page has been turned.

Lenore's Archive grew from that fascination. It is my little corner of the literary world: a place for stories to be examined, ideas to wander, questions to be asked and discoveries to be shared.
